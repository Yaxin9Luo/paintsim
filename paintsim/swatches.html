PaintSim swatches

Every paint is laid with the same brushes on the same ground under the same lamp. The black bars show how well it covers. Click a swatch to move the lamp over it and to change its recipe.

Mixing

Colours are mixed as pigments, by what each absorbs and scatters across the spectrum, not by averaging red, green and blue. Write a mix by parts: color: {mix: {ultramarine: 1, 'cadmium yellow': 2}}. The thin strip under each palette mix is what plain averaging of RGB gives for the same parts.

Wet in wet

Water media stay afloat while they are wet and move when time passes: surface.flow(t) lets them run for a while, surface.dry() lets them finish. Colour travels only where the surface is wet, so wet the paper first (material: 'water') for a bloom, and leave a dry gap to stop one. Oil and chalk do not flow; they mix where a brush works them.

Lighting

The painting is a relief, and the picture is a photograph of it. The same patch under each lighting set-up: surface.light('window'). Changing the light costs one render, not a repaint. The Light menu at the top relights this whole page.
